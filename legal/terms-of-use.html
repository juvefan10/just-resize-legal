Replacing Originals

The app can optionally replace an original photo in your library with its compressed version. This is a destructive action that you must explicitly confirm each time. Compression is lossy: the replacement will not be pixel-identical to the original.

On iOS, the original is removed through your operating system's own deletion prompt and can be recovered from Recently Deleted for 30 days. On Android 11 and later, most photos go through the equivalent system prompt and can be recovered from the device's trash for 30 days. On Android 10 and earlier the app will not replace an original at all, because those versions have no system trash to recover it from, and it refuses rather than deleting anything.

Compression is lossy, so a replacement is never identical to the original. Keep your own backups of anything irreplaceable.
